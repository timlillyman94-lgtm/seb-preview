What is SEB?

Social, emotional and behavioural skills underpin learning.

SocialEmotionalBehavioural

When expectations, routines, acknowledgement and responses to behaviour are consistent across a school, students and staff get safe, positive and predictable learning environments.

We help schools build that consistency into everyday systems at every tier of support, rather than relying on individual teachers or one-off programs.

Tier 3
Intensive
Individualised supportFunctional behaviour assessment, behaviour support planning and de-escalation for students with complex needs.
Tier 2
Targeted
Small-group supportData-based identification and practical interventions, such as Check-In Check-Out, with progress monitoring.
Tier 1
Universal
Whole-school systemsShared expectations, routines, behaviour-specific praise and calm, consistent responses for every student.
